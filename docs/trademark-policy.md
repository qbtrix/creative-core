# Trademark policy

> **Draft, needs legal review before use.** For the reviewer: name the

The code of AfterGlow is free software under GPL-3.0: you may use, change
and share it. The names and icons are different. They tell people that what
they are running is the real AfterGlow, made and supported by us, and this
policy keeps that true. The GPL does not grant trademark rights
(`ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`, term 3).

**The marks:** the names "AfterGlow", "Flare", "Design", "Photo",
"Library" and "Halo" as names of this suite and its apps, and the official
icons and logos of each.

## What you may do without asking

- **Ship unmodified official builds** under their names, with their icons:
  share the official download, mirror it, install it across your company.
- **Build from source without changes** and call it AfterGlow, as long as
  the build is the same source as an official release.
- **Write about it:** articles, reviews, tutorials, books, videos, talks and
  screenshots may use the names and show the icons as they appear in the
  app, to refer to the real AfterGlow.
- **Say what your work is:** a fork may say "based on AfterGlow"; a plugin,
  file or tutorial may say "for AfterGlow" or "works with AfterGlow
  Flare", as long as it doesn't suggest we made or endorse it.
- **Talk about it:** user groups, meetups and community sites may use the
  names to say what they are about.

## What forks and modified builds must do

A build with any change to the code (a fork, a patched build, a build with
other modules) is a different program. It must:

- use its **own name**, not AfterGlow or a name that is easily confused
  with it or with an app name (no "AfterGlow Plus", no "Flare Pro");
- use its **own icons and logos**, not ours or look-alikes;
- not claim to be official, supported or endorsed by us;
- keep the copyright notices and say it is modified (`ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`,
  terms 1 and 2).

It may say, in plain text, that it is "based on AfterGlow".

## What needs our written permission

- Using a mark as part of your product, company, domain or app name.
- Selling or bundling anything under a mark, including official builds
  sold for a price or preinstalled for sale.
- Distributing a modified build under a mark, even with small changes.
- Using the icons or logos in anything other than a screenshot or a plain
  reference to the real app: merchandise, ads, your own app's interface.
- Any use that suggests a partnership, sponsorship or endorsement.

Ask through the maintainers (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`). When in doubt, ask;
most reasonable uses that help people find the real AfterGlow get a yes.
